More ways to share
Beyond the README.

    

      
Link previews
Every city unfurls into its skyline on Slack, Discord, X and LinkedIn. Nothing to set up: just paste the link.

      
Embeds
Drop the live, orbitable city into a page. Add &hud=0 for just the city.

        

      
Moments
In any city, Share → copy this moment gives a link that opens at the same date, speed and camera angle.

        
commit.city/you#t=2026-03-14T21:30&speed=1h

    

    
Card parameters

    

      
tThe user, org or owner/repo. Required.

      
theme=lightLight colours. Leave it off for dark, or use the <picture> snippet above to follow the reader's theme.

      
animate=0No rising towers. Use it where motion would distract.

      
all=1A repo's whole-history city instead of its last year.
